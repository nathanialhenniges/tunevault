---
title: Backup & restore
description: Save your library and bring it back later.
---

## Back up

1. Open **Settings → Library → Backups** (or **File → Back Up Library…**).
2. Choose whether to **include media**:
   - **Off:** saves your tags, playlists and history only. Small and fast.
   - **On:** also copies your music. You'll need enough free space.
3. Click **Export backup…** and pick a folder.

TuneVault creates a `TuneVault-backup-…` folder there. Keep it private, because it lists
your file paths.

## Restore

1. Open **Settings → Library → Restore from backup…**.
2. Choose the `TuneVault-backup-…` folder.
3. Review what will change and confirm.

Before replacing anything, TuneVault saves a **recovery copy** of your current library, so
you can undo a restore.

<Callout type="warn">
  A backup without media can't bring back music files you've deleted. Use **Include media**
  if you want a complete copy.
</Callout>

After a restore, choose each player's export folder again in **Devices & exports**.
