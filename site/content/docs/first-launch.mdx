## 1. Read the notice

The first screen explains that TuneVault is for music you own or have permission to copy.
Read it and click **Agree and continue**. You can read the full
[Terms of Use](/docs/legal/terms-of-use) and [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy-policy)
any time.

## 2. Choose your library folder

TuneVault suggests a folder for you:

- **Mac:** `Music/TuneVault/Library`
- **Windows:** `Music\TuneVault\Library`

Use the suggestion or pick another folder. Everything you add is copied here. You can
change it later in **Settings → Library**.

## 3. Add your music

Click **Add music** (or press <kbd>⌘ O</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl O</kbd> on Windows) and
pick one way:

| Tab | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Local files** | Audio files or whole folders on your computer |
| **Paste a link** | A link to audio you're allowed to save |
| **Playlist file** | A playlist exported from another app (see [Playlists](/docs/playlists)) |

Your originals are copied, never moved or changed.

## Replay the guide

To see the welcome guide again, open **Settings → General → Show welcome guide**.
