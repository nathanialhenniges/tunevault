TuneVault is a desktop music library for **Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 15 or later)** and
**Windows 10/11 PCs**. It keeps your music on your computer and helps you get it onto
portable players.

## What you can do

- **Build a library.** Add audio files or folders. TuneVault copies them into its own
  library folder and never changes your originals.
- **Fix tags and artwork.** Look up the right title, artist, album and cover, then approve
  each change before it's saved.
- **Keep ordered playlists.** Make your own, or import one from another app.
- **Prepare music for a player.** Export compatible files for an iPod, USB stick, SD card
  or any folder.
- **Back up everything.** Save your library to a folder and restore it later.

<Callout title="For music you own" type="warn">
  TuneVault is for audio you own or have permission to copy. Saving a song from a link is
  only allowed when the service's terms and the law let you.
</Callout>
