---
title: Tags and artwork
description: Look up missing details and covers online.
---

TuneVault can suggest the right title, artist, album and cover for a track. It never
changes a file on its own: you approve every change.

## Find suggestions

1. Open a track's **Track details**.
2. Under **Metadata suggestions**, click **Find suggestions**.
3. The first time, TuneVault explains that a lookup sends your search terms and IP address
   to the service. Confirm to continue.
4. Pick the right match, review the changes, and apply them.

Fields you've **locked** are never overwritten.

## Where suggestions come from

| Service | Gives you |
| --- | --- |
| [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org) | Titles, artists, albums, track numbers |
| [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org) | Album covers |

Both are free community services, so no account or API key is needed.

## Turning it off

In **Settings → Sources → Metadata & artwork**, you can turn off each service or all online
lookups. Your library keeps working fully offline.
