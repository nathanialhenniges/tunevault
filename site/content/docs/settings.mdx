---
title: Settings
description: What each settings tab does.
---

Open Settings with <kbd>⌘ ,</kbd> on Mac or <kbd>Ctrl ,</kbd> on Windows.

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Appearance (System, Light or Dark), accent colour (Blue or Orange), and the welcome guide |
| **Library** | Library folder, backups and restore |
| **Sources** | Online metadata and artwork services, and the optional cookie credentials for links |
| **Updates** | Check for updates |
| **Privacy** | What's stored on your computer, your agreements, and the legal documents |
| **Advanced** | Bundled tool versions, support report and design fixtures |

Picking the **Orange** accent also switches the app logo to orange.
