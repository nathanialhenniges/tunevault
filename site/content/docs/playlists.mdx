## Make a playlist

1. Open **Playlists** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create playlist** and give it a name.
3. In **Library**, select tracks and choose **Add to playlist**.

Order is kept exactly, and the same song can appear more than once.

## Import a playlist

Use **Add music → Playlist file**. TuneVault reads:

- **M3U / M3U8**
- **CSV / TSV** with columns `title`, `artist`, `album`, `duration` and an optional `url`
- **Spotify** playlist JSON exports
- **Apple Music** XML or text exports

Imported entries are matched to songs already in your library. Anything TuneVault isn't
sure about is shown for you to review: you can match it to a local file, pick an exact
source you're allowed to use, or skip it.

<Callout>
  An imported playlist only lists songs. It doesn't download music from Spotify or Apple
  Music.
</Callout>
