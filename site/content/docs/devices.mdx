## Add a player

1. Open **Devices & exports** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add export target**.
3. Pick the kind of player and a name, such as "Travel player".
4. Choose where files go. TuneVault suggests a folder under `Music/TuneVault/Exports`.

| Player type | What TuneVault makes |
| --- | --- |
| iPod (via iMazing) | MP3, AAC or ALAC files plus playlists, ready to import with iMazing |
| USB stick / SD card / folder | MP3 files (192 kbps when conversion is needed) plus M3U8 playlists |
| Archive | The original format, or FLAC |
| Android | A folder to copy over yourself |

## Export

1. Choose what to send: tracks or playlists.
2. Click **Review export**. TuneVault shows how many songs can be prepared, how much space
   they need, and anything it has to skip.
3. Click **Prepare reviewed files**.
4. Click **Check files** to confirm the copies match.

Files that are already compatible are copied as they are, so quality isn't lost twice.

## Things to know

- TuneVault only writes to the folder you chose. It never deletes files it didn't create.
- Keep the whole export folder together: later playlists can reuse files from earlier
  exports.
- **iPod:** import the prepared folder with iMazing, check your iPod, then click
  **Confirm transfer to this iPod** in TuneVault.
